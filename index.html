About

I'm an undergraduate at IIT Jodhpur studying Engineering Science with majors in Computer Science and Electrical Engineering. My research sits where stochastic modeling meets real systems: market making under model ambiguity, leakage-controlled financial NLP, parameter-efficient test-time adaptation, and the geometry of open-set recognition.

I care about results that survive adversarial evaluation: purged cross-validation, embargoed splits, and reproducibility packages, not just results that look good in-sample. Previously, I worked on quantitative research at WorldQuant BRAIN and completed a quant data engineering internship at Detachmint Ventures.
